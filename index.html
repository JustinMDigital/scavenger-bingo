Rally Hunt

Rally Hunt is a browser-based scavenger hunt app that lets anyone create and run a temporary group game, share a room code, and let players join teams and complete a live game board without creating accounts.

After a hunt, hosts and authorized players can create a presentation of a finished team board in their chosen Google Drive account. Rally Hunt uses Google Drive only to create and access presentations made through that requested export; it does not browse unrelated Drive files.

How Rally Hunt works

  1. Hosts choose a ready-made hunt or create teams, prompts, timing, and a route.
  2. Players join from their phones with a shared room code and no player account.
  3. Teams complete a live board, optionally submit photos, and review the finish.

Temporary by design

Proof photos are optional, and temporary room data and uploaded photos are removed after seven days.
